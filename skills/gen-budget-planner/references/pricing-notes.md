Patterns
- Resolution usually scales price 2–4× per step. Audio on often adds 30–60 %.
- Image-to-video costs the same as text-to-video on several models and gives far better character consistency.
- Voice lines are cheap enough to do a full reel for single-digit credits. Video is where budgets go.
- "Unlimited" windows and free-trial allowances are separate balances. Never switch one on for the owner.
